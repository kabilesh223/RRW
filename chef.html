Meet the Master

Chef Antoine Laurent

With over 20 years of experience in Michelin-starred kitchens across Paris, Tokyo, and New York, Chef Antoine brings a global perspective to every dish he creates. His philosophy is rooted in respect for the ingredient — letting nature's best shine with minimal intervention and maximum flavour.

Trained at Le Cordon Bleu Paris and refined under legendary chefs Alain Ducasse and Gordon Ramsay, Antoine's cuisine is a seamless blend of classical French rigour and contemporary creativity.

"Cooking is not just about feeding people — it's about creating moments of joy, connection, and wonder at the table."

The Lumière Way

Culinary Philosophy

Three principles that guide everything we create.

Respect the Ingredient

Every component is chosen for its peak quality and provenance. We never mask a great ingredient — we elevate it through thoughtful technique and perfect pairing.

Technique with Purpose

Classical French training forms our foundation. But technique exists to serve the dish — every method chosen to bring out the truest, most intense possible flavour.

Emotion at the Table

A meal at Lumière should feel like a memory before it's even over. We obsess over atmosphere, pacing, and the details that make guests feel genuinely cared for.

Learn from the Best

Chef's Masterclass

Twice a month, Chef Antoine opens the Lumière kitchen to small groups for an exclusive hands-on cooking masterclass. Learn the techniques behind our most celebrated dishes in the very space where they were created.

Each session ends with a full sit-down dinner of everything you cooked, paired with wines selected by sommelier Sofia Reyes.

  • ◆Max 8 participants · 3 hours hands-on
  • ◆Full dinner & wine pairing included
  • ◆Printed recipe booklet to take home
  • ◆Available every first & third Saturday
