Got Questions?

Offer FAQs

Can I combine multiple offers in a single visit?

Offers cannot be combined with one another, but they can be used alongside your Loyalty Programme points for maximum value.

Do I need to book in advance to use an offer?

Yes, we recommend booking at least 48 hours in advance and mentioning the offer so our team can prepare. Walk-ins are welcome but subject to availability.

Is the Birthday Celebration available for the whole month?

The birthday offer applies to your actual birthday date. Please bring a valid photo ID. We'll gladly arrange a surprise if you're celebrating for someone else.

How far in advance do I need to book the Chef's Table experience?

The Chef's Table seats only 8 guests and is available on Friday and Saturday evenings. We recommend booking at least 2 weeks in advance due to high demand.
