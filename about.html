Our Story

A Legacy of Culinary
Excellence

Founded in the heart of the city, Lumière began as a dream to bring world-class dining to our community. Today, we blend traditional techniques with modern innovation to create dishes that celebrate the finest local and international ingredients.

Every plate is a canvas, every meal an experience designed to delight your senses and create lasting memories with those you cherish most. Our team of dedicated chefs sources the freshest seasonal produce daily to craft menus that evolve with nature's calendar.

Our Journey

Milestones & History

2008

Lumière Opens Its Doors

Chef Antoine Laurent unveils Lumière in downtown Manhattan, quickly gaining a loyal following for its bold French-inspired menu and intimate atmosphere.

2011

First Michelin Recognition

Lumière receives its first Michelin star, cementing its place among New York's elite dining destinations and attracting guests from around the world.

2014

Wine Cellar Expansion

We curate our award-winning cellar of 400+ labels, partnering with boutique vineyards across France, Italy, and the New World to complement our evolving menu.

2017

Private Dining Suite Launch

Our dedicated private dining rooms open for corporate events, weddings, and exclusive celebrations — each designed to offer the full Lumière experience in an intimate setting.

2020

Farm Partnership Program

Lumière formalises relationships with seven certified organic farms across New York state, guaranteeing daily fresh deliveries and full traceability for every ingredient.

2024

Chef's Table Experience

We introduce the exclusive Chef's Table — an intimate 8-seat experience where guests watch the kitchen in action while enjoying a custom 9-course tasting menu.
