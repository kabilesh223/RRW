Our Story

A Legacy of Culinary
Excellence

Founded in the heart of the city, Lumière began as a dream to bring world-class dining to our community. Today, we blend traditional techniques with modern innovation to create dishes that celebrate the finest local and international ingredients.

Every plate is a canvas, every meal an experience designed to delight your senses and create lasting memories with those you cherish most. Our team of dedicated chefs sources the freshest seasonal produce daily to craft menus that evolve with nature's calendar.

Meet the Master

Chef Antoine Laurent

With over 20 years of experience in Michelin-starred kitchens across Paris, Tokyo, and New York, Chef Antoine brings a global perspective to every dish he creates. His philosophy is rooted in respect for the ingredient — letting nature's best shine with minimal intervention and maximum flavour.

Trained at Le Cordon Bleu Paris and refined under legendary chefs Alain Ducasse and Gordon Ramsay, Antoine's cuisine is a seamless blend of classical French rigour and contemporary creativity.

"Cooking is not just about feeding people — it's about creating moments of joy, connection, and wonder at the table."
